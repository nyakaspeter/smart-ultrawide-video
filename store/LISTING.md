# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name

Smart Ultrawide Video

## Summary

Auto-detects and removes black bars from fullscreen videos

## Category

Tools

## Language

English

## Detailed description

Make fullscreen video fit ultrawide, super-ultrawide, and portrait displays
without blindly stretching the picture.

Smart Ultrawide Video analyzes video frames locally, detects black bars baked
into the image, and applies the smallest appropriate zoom while the video is
fullscreen. It adapts when the fullscreen viewport changes and restores the
page's original video styling when fullscreen ends.

Features:

- Automatic black-bar detection for fullscreen HTML5 video
- Works with landscape, ultrawide, super-ultrawide, and portrait viewports
- Never applies a zoom below 100%
- Adjustable frame-analysis frequency and zoom-change tolerance
- Configurable zoom-out delay and optional zoom animation
- Toolbar on/off control and Alt+Shift+U keyboard shortcut
- No analysis or visual modification outside fullscreen
- No accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or network service

Compatibility depends on the website exposing a readable HTML5 video frame.
Some DRM-protected or cross-origin players may prevent browser extensions from
reading pixels; in that case Smart Ultrawide Video leaves the video unchanged.

## Suggested support text

If a player is not detected correctly, include the website, browser version,
display resolution, video resolution, and whether the content is DRM-protected
in your support report. Do not include account credentials or private video
content.
